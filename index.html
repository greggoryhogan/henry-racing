Built for Speed. Backed by American Craftsmanship.

The Henry Racing Team is a Wisconsin-based performance-driven motorsports initiative rooted in precision, reliability, and American ingenuity. Inspired by the same commitment to quality and craftsmanship that defines our heritage, we bring that mindset to the track where performance matters and every detail counts.

About the team

The Henry Racing Team was established to celebrate the spirit of American motorsports and the communities that keep it alive. From grassroots racing to competitive circuits, our focus is simple: show up prepared, perform with purpose, and represent the values of hard work, integrity, and excellence.

We partner with drivers, builders, and racing organizations who share our passion for precision engineering and relentless improvement. Whether it’s on asphalt, dirt, or anything in between, we believe in earning every inch.

What drives us

  • Performance First
    Built to compete, tuned to win.
  • American-Made Mindset
    A commitment to quality, durability, and doing things the right way.
  • Grassroots Focus
    Supporting local tracks, local talent, and the next generation of racers.
  • Precision & Reliability
    Because performance means nothing without consistency.

Where to find us

The Henry Racing Team participates in regional racing events, community showcases, and motorsports gatherings across the country. We’re proud to connect with fans, families, and fellow enthusiasts who share a passion for speed, engineering, and competition.
